Consolidated Billing Automation

Performance Systems Integration · June 2022 to October 2022, then handed off

Summary

I redesigned and automated an inherited, labor-intensive monthly billing workflow for 13 client locations using Sage Intacct Smart Events. After taking responsibility for the manual process in June 2022, I had the automation and its supporting business process established by October 2022. The solution dynamically paired qualifying invoices to the correct parent invoice and reduced monthly effort from approximately 40 hours to approximately 1.5 hours while preserving final validation for completeness and duplicate billing.

Context and problem

The client needed one consolidated invoice per month for each location rather than hundreds of individual invoices. The original process meant creating a master invoice for every location, running location-specific reports, finding each individual invoice, manually assigning its parent invoice, and then completing the master invoice.

After ServiceTrade was introduced, invoices also had to be verified and pushed to Sage Intacct. Staff closed jobs, checked that work had not been billed twice, and then repeated the same parent-invoice process. The combined workload contributed to a billing backlog.

Architecture

What I built

Solution design

  • Mapped the manual workflow and identified Sage Intacct Smart Events as the automation mechanism; available Sage Intacct API volume was constrained at the time, so the automation lives entirely in Smart Events.
  • Configured logic for each billing month, the following month, and each applicable client location.
  • Evaluated newly created ERP invoices against client and location criteria and dynamically assigned matching invoices to the appropriate parent invoice.
  • Reduced the recurring manual work to creating the master invoices, updating dates and master-invoice numbers in the Smart Events, and completing final validation.

Validation, controls, and handoff

  • Verified that every qualifying invoice was assigned to a parent invoice and checked for duplicate billing before completing the cycle; the final master invoice was completed only after those checks passed.
  • Kept human validation for the highest-risk steps rather than treating automation as proof of correctness.
  • Trained the next process owner over several months so the workflow could continue reliably as my responsibilities shifted toward data analytics.

Related integration improvement

Separately, with Sage Intacct API capacity limited, I identified inefficient webhook behavior in a third-party Salesforce-to-Sage Intacct integration and challenged the partner's design. The partner reconfigured its webhook usage, reducing Sage Intacct API consumption by nearly half.

Ownership

Personally designed and built

  • The Smart Events automation, the supporting business process, and the end-of-cycle controls.
  • The diagnosis and recommendation behind the webhook reconfiguration.
  • Training and transition to the next process owner.

Inherited or done by others

  • The original master-invoice process existed before me; I took responsibility for running it in June 2022 and then automated it.
  • The webhook change itself was implemented by the third-party integration partner following my diagnosis.
  • No AI coding tools were involved; this predates my use of them.
