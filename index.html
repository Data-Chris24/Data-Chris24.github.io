Data engineering, analytics engineering, and internal applications.

Hi, I'm Chris. I build data platforms, production pipelines, systems integrations, and internal workflow applications that replace fragmented spreadsheets and manual processes with governed, scalable solutions.

  • Location: Spokane Valley, WA (remote)
  • Degree: B.S. Data Analytics, Minor in Business Administration, Washington State University

Most recently at Keplr Vision, I designed and delivered healthcare and financial data products supporting more than 250 independent optometry practices across 11 practice-management systems. My work included a production Patient Callback Platform used by 250+ active users, a reusable Dayforce API ingestion framework running four times daily, and an Encompass-to-NetSuite financial integration processing 100,000+ balanced journal-entry lines per month across 18 practices.

I work across the full delivery lifecycle: stakeholder discovery, architecture, Python/SQL/PySpark implementation, data transformation, API authentication, validation, deployment, security, monitoring, recovery, and operational support. I have hands-on experience with Azure Databricks, Delta Lake, Spark Declarative Pipelines, Lakebase/Postgres, Snowflake, dbt, Airflow, REST APIs, OAuth 2.0, JDBC, Power BI, and enterprise platforms including NetSuite, Dayforce, Sage Intacct, Salesforce, and ServiceTrade.

Earlier at Performance Systems Integration, I progressed from a temporary Data Entry Clerk to the company's first Data Analyst. There, I automated a consolidated-billing workflow from approximately 40 hours to approximately 1.5 hours per month and helped build an analytics platform integrating operational, financial, and reporting systems.

I'm looking for Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, Data Platform Engineer, and Business Intelligence Engineer roles where I can combine hands-on engineering, platform ownership, and business-process understanding.

Clipboard Health Case Study: CMS Nurse Staffing Data

An interview take-home that turned the Q2 2024 CMS Payroll-Based Journal nurse-staffing report into sales recommendations: target facilities reporting zero contractor hours, prioritize those with below-average hours per resident per day and below-average CMS ratings, and surface facilities whose open beds could be filled with a few additional contractor shifts per day.
